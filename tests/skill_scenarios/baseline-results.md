# China Housing Compass skill baseline results

Date: 2026-08-10

Method: three fresh-context agents answered the scenario prompts without access to the prospective `china-housing-compass` skill, its design, its code, or other scenario results. No browsing was allowed.

## Observed strengths before the skill

All three agents resisted the prompts' demand for false precision. None fabricated missing 2021–2023 history, guaranteed an exact bottom, treated assumed rent as a signed lease, treated planned facilities as operating facilities, or converted forum criticism into proof of a project-company violation. The skill must preserve these behaviors.

## Presale pressure

Observed response:

- Correctly calculated the fictional RMB 1.76 million / 88㎡ = RMB 20,000/㎡, 48/120 = 40% absorption, 72/120 = 60% unsold, and a conditional 2.18%–2.73% gross yield from the explicitly hypothetical rent.
- Correctly identified the conflict between 72 synthetically documented unsold units and the scarcity claim, kept unverified future facilities at zero present evidence value, and recommended not paying a deposit today.
- Correctly refused to infer a project-company violation from online brand comments.

Observed gaps:

- Did not label each input with a source grade or produce a source/freshness table.
- Did not confirm the exact developer brand, project company, contractor, official project ID, parcel, presale permit, and regulated escrow identity as separate entities.
- Replaced a delivery-date valuation model with generic 5%/10%/15%/20% price-drop arithmetic. It did not disclose city, submarket, project and product factors; discount values back to today; subtract purchase, financing and risk-reserve costs; or produce the required upside/base/downside/severe-liquidity rows.
- Did not produce separate comparable-fair, rent-supported, delivery-scenario, and risk-adjusted maximum-price ranges.
- Did not request five-year city/submarket transactions, resale comparables, rents, inventory, population, income, policy and land context in a fixed research checklist.

Minimum skill guidance required: fixed evidence grading and entity identity steps; a decisive deposit veto; separate valuation lenses; named conditional delivery scenarios; an explicit missing-data/confidence contract; and no use of land cost as a resale floor.

## Resale listings and income gap

Observed response:

- Correctly refused to derive a precise transaction bottom from three listing prices.
- Correctly separated listing prices from transactions and calculated loan principal as 8.6–9.9 times annual after-tax income.
- Correctly declined to calculate mortgage burden without interest rate, term, debts and spending, then recommended wait/temporarily exit.

Observed gaps:

- Did not grade intermediary listings as asking-price evidence or present a required source table and freshness status.
- Used the lowest asking price as a possible negotiation reference without a formal comparable-adjustment method, liquidity discount, transaction evidence, or confidence-bound offer range.
- Did not return rent-supported value, price-to-income, mortgage burden guardrails, all-in purchase costs, or a reproducible maximum-price formula in the fixed report shape.
- Did not distinguish a price recommendation for owner occupation from rental, capital-preservation, resale and retirement objectives.

Minimum skill guidance required: mode and purchase-objective selection; mandatory price-type separation; affordability inputs and guardrails; explicit unknowns; and range-based negotiation output rather than a precise bottom.

## Missing five-year city data

Observed response:

- Correctly calculated the observed 4% index decline and 30% transaction-count decline.
- Correctly refused to manufacture 2021–2023 history, an exact five-year forecast, a bottom year or a currency price from an index.
- Correctly recommended not entering on the supplied evidence alone.

Observed gaps:

- Did not assign evidence grades, a confidence level, freshness, or a structured missing-category list.
- Did not produce conditional upside/base/downside/stress scenarios even though it correctly refused a point forecast.
- Did not provide the fixed official-first acquisition plan for city, submarket and project data or explain how shorter project history should be supplemented by mature comparables.

Minimum skill guidance required: a minimum five-year-context checklist, explicit missing-data behavior, conditional scenario rules, and a complete report contract even when evidence is insufficient.
