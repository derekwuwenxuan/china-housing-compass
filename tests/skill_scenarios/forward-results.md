# China Housing Compass forward-test results

Date: 2026-08-10

Method: three fresh-context agents read the completed skill and only the references routed by it, then answered the same baseline prompts. They did not see baseline results, the implementation plan, design spec, source code, or other scenario answers. No browsing was allowed. Two scenarios were rerun after a demonstrated instruction refinement.

## Presale pressure: pass after one refinement

Initial forward result correctly:

- separated decision confidence from the inability to prove a price bottom;
- kept developer quote, official inventory summary, field observations, rent assumptions and online comments distinct;
- calculated the fictional RMB 20,000/㎡, 40% absorption, 60% unsold and the conditional 2.18%–2.73% gross yield;
- treated developer-only future facilities at zero present value;
- refused to attribute brand sentiment to the project company;
- made “wait / no deposit today” decisive;
- returned all 12 report sections and named upside/base/downside/stress plus delay/quality sensitivities;
- called the stress low value conditional rather than an exact bottom.

Demonstrated gap: the first run called a user-described saved official page grade A despite having no inspectable title/URL. The skill was tightened to require grade C pending verification or “claimed A, unverified” until the primary source identity, scope and date are inspectable. The rerun applied this exactly and separately reported **high decision confidence** and **low valuation confidence**.

The rerun also generated a disclosed illustrative screening range only after listing its arbitrary factor assumptions and explicitly denied that it was fair value. This satisfies sensitivity analysis, not a current purchase valuation.

## Resale income gap: pass after one refinement

The forward result correctly:

- labeled RMB 2.25m–2.48m as listing range only, not a fair range or bottom;
- calculated asking unit prices, price-to-income, down-payment share and loan need;
- refused to fabricate mortgage payment without rate/term/cash-flow inputs;
- left transaction, rent-supported, maximum-price and stress values unavailable;
- returned the fixed report, high-confidence wait decision and a verification checklist.

Demonstrated clarity gap: the initial response used one high-confidence label even though valuation evidence was weak. The skill was refined to separate decision and valuation confidence. The rerun reported **decision confidence high** and **valuation confidence low**.

## Missing city data: pass

The forward result correctly:

- retained missing 2021–2023 cells instead of filling history;
- calculated only the observed -4% index and -30% transaction changes;
- explained why an index cannot create a currency price;
- assigned low confidence and listed every missing source category;
- returned non-numerical upside/base/downside/severe-stress conditions rather than manufactured forecasts;
- kept all price ranges unavailable and gave a prioritized official-first acquisition plan;
- ended with wait and the non-guaranteed-bottom boundary.

## Contract comparison

All final scenario outputs:

- kept filing/quote/listing/transaction/rent types separate;
- showed evidence grades and freshness limits;
- did not count planned facilities as operating;
- did not promote comments to official developer/project facts;
- did not fabricate missing history, rent, transactions or probabilities;
- returned recommendation, decisive conditions, identity gaps, calculations, five-year context, affordability, scenarios, sources and next actions;
- used ranges/conditional unavailability instead of a precise guaranteed bottom.

Result: **3/3 scenarios pass after evidence-driven refinement.**
