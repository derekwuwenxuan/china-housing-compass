# Three-video real-estate framework

Use these as analytical lenses, not as a promise that one formula discovers intrinsic value.

## Video 1: land and developer cost pressure

All currency is RMB.

```text
chargeable gross floor area = land area × floor-area ratio
floor land price (RMB/㎡) = land transaction total / chargeable gross floor area
land-to-home-price ratio = floor land price / home sale unit price
```

Example: 10,000㎡ of land, FAR 3.0 and RMB 300 million consideration give 30,000㎡ chargeable GFA and RMB 10,000/㎡ floor land price. If the home sells at RMB 40,000/㎡, the ratio is 25%.

Interpretation: a lower ratio may mean more developer margin and price flexibility; a higher ratio may mean margin pressure, reduced quality, or limited discount room. It does **not** directly prove housing value or profit because construction, tax, finance, marketing, public-facility, relocation and other costs remain.

```text
project profit = sales revenue
                 - land cost
                 - construction and installation
                 - taxes
                 - financing
                 - sales/marketing
                 - other project obligations
```

Never treat floor land price as a buyer's resale floor.

## Video 2: policy and public support

This is qualitative. There is no defensible universal formula for “government willingness to support a property.” Examine actual policy text, implementation scope, funding, eligibility, dates, land/supply actions and infrastructure construction. Distinguish city support, submarket policy and protection of a specific project. Never infer guaranteed price support.

## Video 3: rent support

```text
gross rental yield = annual gross rent / all-in acquisition cost
net rental yield = (annual rent - vacancy - repairs - owner costs - taxes) / all-in cost
rent-supported price = annual rent / target yield
```

Use actual comparable contract or observed asking rents when available and distinguish them. Target yield must reflect locality, liquidity, vacancy, costs and alternatives; do not import a universal “international healthy yield.” An assumed RMB 3,500–4,500 rent produces scenario values only.

## Comment handling

Quote only comments actually captured and preserve the capture date/source. Otherwise write “comment theme summary, not verbatim comments.” Comments can surface hypotheses — low yield, special Chinese housing attributes, core-city scarcity, future cash-flow importance — but do not establish transaction facts, laws or violations.
