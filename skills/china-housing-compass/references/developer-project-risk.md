# Developer, project, product and infrastructure risk

## Resolve entities

Keep separate:

- marketing/developer brand;
- legal project company and shareholders;
- land holder;
- general contractor, design and supervision entities;
- property manager;
- specific phase, permit, building and unit.

Check official corporate, permit, court, enforcement, penalty and construction records. A brand's reputation may justify more research but is not proof against the project company. Link every adverse fact to entity, event, date, authority, status and project relevance.

## Deposit vetoes

**Wait / do not deposit until resolved:**

- official unsold inventory materially conflicts with “last unit” urgency;
- the exact unit and regulated availability cannot be verified;
- a promised model home/product/fit-out cannot be inspected;
- garden, usable area, parking, fit-out, school or facility rights are verbal only;
- delivery schedule or construction evidence is materially inadequate;
- the deposit's refund conditions are unclear.

**Walk away when verified:**

- payment is requested outside the regulated escrow/payment path;
- material buyer rights are excluded from the contract;
- illegality, permit failure or enforceability problems defeat the purchase;
- identity or contract documents are materially falsified.

## Delivery/product checks

Inspect or verify progress by building, funding/escrow, permit scope, contractual delivery date, fit-out schedule, inspection/acceptance path, delay compensation, force-majeure clauses, area tolerances, garden rights, common areas, parking and property fee. A building not topped out is not automatically fraudulent; it raises construction and delivery evidence requirements.

## Infrastructure realization

- operating: full present utility;
- under construction: partial conditional value with evidence and schedule;
- approved/funded: lower conditional value;
- conceptual: scenario only;
- developer-only: zero present realization value.

Record real walking/access conditions, physical barriers, noise, industry, slopes, roads, transit, supermarkets, wet markets, schools, hospitals and parks. Do not count a distant administrative boundary label as convenient access.
