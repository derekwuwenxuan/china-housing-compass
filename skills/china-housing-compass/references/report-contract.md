# Fixed assessment report contract

Return all thirteen sections in this order, even when evidence is missing.

## 1. Decision

- recommendation: **buy**, **negotiate**, **wait**, or **walk away**;
- decision confidence: high, medium, or low;
- valuation confidence: high, medium, or low when different from decision confidence;
- one-sentence reason;
- decisive deposit conditions or vetoes.

## 2. Exact identity

Show the property/community, phase/building/unit, area basis and status; developer brand, legal project company, contractor, official project/permit/parcel and escrow identity. Mark each unverified field.

## 3. Price types now

Show filing/official price, developer quote/net price, Lianjia/Beike asking-price distribution, Lianjia/Beike platform-reported transaction distribution, verified resale transactions, judicial-auction appraisal/opening/winning prices, bank appraisal, other appraisal if any, asking rents and verified/contract rents in a table. For sufficiently matched platform samples show mean, median and lower quantile. Never combine them into one “market price.”

Add a **platform coverage matrix** for resale and rent: platform, metric/record type, access mode, attempted/obtained status, raw/deduplicated sample counts, observed/retrieved dates, freshness and caveat. Show Lianjia and Beike attempts explicitly. State when fewer than two independent intermediary platforms produced usable, metric-matched evidence.

For a user-supplied platform screenshot, identify `user_supplied_screenshot`, show asking and platform-reported transaction averages separately, state whether the sample count and underlying records are inspectable, and label any area conversion as an `area-equivalent scenario`.

## 4. Valuation ranges

- comparable fair range and adjustments;
- rent-supported range with target yield and costs;
- delivery-date scenario range;
- risk-adjusted maximum purchase price today;
- buyer all-in cost and negotiation ceiling;
- downside and severe-stress loss band.
- dual-anchor alignment: `aligned`, `diverging`, or `insufficient_evidence`, with the overlap/gap explained.
- judicial-auction cross-check with matched-market adjustments and bank-appraisal cross-check when available.

Label unavailable lenses instead of substituting another price type.

## 5. Formula worksheet

Show inputs, units, dates, assumptions and calculations for applicable land-to-home, rent, affordability, premium, absorption, inventory, judicial-auction winning discount and failed-auction rate formulas. Explain limitations.

## 6. Five-year context

Show city, submarket and comparable indices, prices/transactions, rents, listing liquidity, supply/inventory/absorption, distinct judicial-auction assets/outcomes, land, population, income/employment, mortgage and policy. List missing years and categories without fabrication.

Then show a one-to-three-year table with year-1, year-2 and year-3 **base**, **weak** and **stress** paths when evidence permits. State policy/economy, demographics/household formation, income/affordability/leverage, rates/credit, supply/stock/absorption, local-government/developer finance, delivery competition and infrastructure assumptions. Separate implemented policy from announced intent and disclose the forecast date. Keep Kondratiev-cycle narratives low weight; keep user-supplied price or market-direction forecasts attributed, case-scoped and zero-weight by default.

## 7. Developer, contract, construction, and delivery

Separate entity-specific official adverse records from sentiment. Show construction, escrow and permit progress; contract, garden, fit-out and delay rights; delivery timing and delay or quality cases.

## 8. Social reputation and captured comments

Show platforms, search time window, actual independent-post and visible-comment counts, access modes, requested-versus-obtained coverage and unavailable sources. State the commercial or coordinated-content caveat. Summarize representative captured themes with dissent and disagreement. Separate unverified allegations from primary-record verification.

When no comments were inspected, report zero captured comments and omit comment quotations, sentiment percentages, invented themes and consensus language.

## 9. Parcel history, environment, infrastructure, and cultural acceptance

Separate exact-parcel, within-500 m, within-1 km and broader-area history. Show historical-use finding state, physical/environmental evidence, remediation or acceptance status, infrastructure realization, residual uncertainty and valuation treatment.

Separate objective physical risk from cultural preference or feng-shui acceptance. Translate cultural factors into user fit, negotiation friction, target-buyer narrowing or resale-liquidity sensitivity; do not assert supernatural harm or a universal discount.

## 10. Affordability

Show down payment, loan, rate/term, monthly payment, income, other debt, living surplus, emergency reserve, price-to-income, mortgage burden and all-in cash needed. State missing inputs.

## 11. Conditional scenarios

Show upside, base, downside and severe liquidity stress; add delay or quality-dispute cases as needed. Disclose factors, delivery value, today's maximum price and conditional loss. Do not assign unsupported probabilities.

Treat each user forecast as attributed, dated, case-scoped, non-transferable and zero-weight by default. Include it only as an explicitly labeled scenario for the same property when requested.

## 12. Evidence, sampling, and freshness

Show metric or finding, geographic/property scope, observed and retrieved dates, grade A–D, URL/title, access mode when applicable and caveat. State intermediary platform coverage, raw/deduplicated samples, unavailable Lianjia/Beike attempts, social sample coverage, finding states, last successful refresh, partial failures and conflicts. For aggregate-only screenshots, state `sample_count: unknown`, raw-capture privacy treatment, structural dependence and whether the unresolved conflict makes a lens `insufficient_evidence`.

## 13. Missing evidence and next actions

Prioritize the missing-source checklist, deposit prerequisites, negotiation conditions and the facts that would change the recommendation. End with: conditional range, not a guaranteed bottom or investment promise.
